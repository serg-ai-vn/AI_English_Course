# Classroom AI Use Agreement

*A short promise every student signs at the end of Lesson 1, before touching any AI tool.*

## For the teacher

Read each line aloud in simple English, check understanding, then let students sign. Written at A2 reading level on purpose — this should take 5 minutes, not feel like a legal document.

**Important**: students sign with their **class nickname** (the same pseudonym system used everywhere else in the course), not their real name — this keeps the paper consistent with how the rest of the course protects their data. The teacher/center keeps the real name ↔ nickname list separately and privately; it never enters the AI-facing system.

---

## My AI Classroom Promise

1. **I will use AI as a helper, not a replacement for my own thinking.** I stay the operator — AI is my co-pilot.

2. **I will never type my real name, my address, my school's name, or my phone number into the AI.** I will use my digital mask instead — a short description, not my real details.

3. **I will never upload a real photo of my face.** I can describe myself in words instead, and let AI draw a picture from my description.

4. **If the AI tells me something surprising, I will check if it's true before I believe it.** AI can be confidently wrong.

5. **I will only use the AI tools my teacher gives me in class** — not other apps or websites.

6. **I will be kind about my classmates' work**, even when their AI results look very different from mine.

7. **I understand my prompts and my work are saved**, so my teacher can help me and see how I'm improving.
